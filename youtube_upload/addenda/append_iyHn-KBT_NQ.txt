THE GOSPEL OF JESUS CHRIST — SALVATION BY GRACE ALONE

The Apostle Paul declares the Gospel in 1 Corinthians 15:3–4. Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures. He was buried and was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures.

God is perfectly holy and righteous. All humanity has sinned against Him and stands guilty before His judgment (Romans 3:10–23). The wages of sin is death. No person can earn forgiveness or eternal life through good works, moral improvement, or religious observances (Romans 6:23).

Yet God, in His great mercy and love, sent His eternal Son into the world. Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He lived a perfectly sinless life and fulfilled the righteousness that we could never achieve.

On the cross, Christ willingly died as the substitute for sinners. He bore the penalty of sin and satisfied God's righteous judgment. His sacrifice was complete and sufficient once for all. On the third day, He rose bodily from the dead. His resurrection demonstrated His victory over sin and death (Isaiah 53:5–6; Romans 4:25; Hebrews 10:10–14).

Salvation is by God's grace alone through faith alone in Jesus Christ alone.

God justifies the ungodly who place their faith in Christ. He forgives their sins and credits Christ's righteousness to them. We are declared righteous before God because of what Christ has accomplished, not because of anything we have done (Romans 3:21–28; Romans 4:5; Philippians 3:9).

Baptism cannot earn justification. Neither can the Lord's Supper, priestly absolution, church membership, religious ceremonies, or good works. No sacrament or human mediator can replace or supplement Christ's finished work.

God commands all people to repent and believe the Gospel (Mark 1:15; Acts 17:30). Turn from sin and abandon every attempt to establish your own righteousness before God. Trust in Jesus Christ alone for forgiveness and eternal life.

True saving faith produces repentance, love, and obedience through the work of the Holy Spirit. These are the fruits of salvation. They are never the cause or foundation of our justification (Ephesians 2:8–10).

Jesus Christ is the only Savior. There is no salvation in anyone else (Acts 4:12; John 14:6).

Repent and believe in the Lord Jesus Christ. Rest entirely upon His perfect righteousness, His atoning death, and His victorious resurrection.

Soli Deo Gloria — To God alone be the glory.
